Security categories refer to cybersecurity and information security.
Prefer the most specific category supported by the main subject: an account of a
concrete attack belongs to security incident; a flaw or patch advisory belongs to
vulnerability; analysis of actors, malware, or attack techniques belongs to threat
intelligence. Use security news for remaining cybersecurity coverage and general
news for other substantive news. Use other for non-news content or insufficient context.
Disinformation identifies coverage of disinformation, misinformation, propaganda,
fact-checking, or influence campaigns. It does not assert that the submitted text
is false or establish deceptive intent; do not infer this label from disagreement,
political viewpoints, or unsupported suspicions.
